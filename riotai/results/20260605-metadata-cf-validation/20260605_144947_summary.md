## Summary and Key Takeaway

- 21 of 30 datasets passed, and the `100-149` bin passed cleanly.
- The failures are concentrated in the `50-99` bin and mostly reduce to one repeated ECMWF HighResMIP `pr` issue: kerchunk exposes 618 time steps while NetCDF exposes 780, so the kerchunk time axis is truncated or shifted. See [ecmwf_time_report.md](../../../ecmwf_time_report.md).
- The remaining non-pass cases are:
  1. filename-resolution issue for the ECMWF `hus` kerchunk reference
  2. publication/provenance drift for HAMMOZ `hur`, with no CF-significant attribute differences
  3. one intentional skip for a dataset that stalls during the data check
- No failures point to xCDAT CF-axis detection.
- In this sample, xCDAT does not show evidence of a broad kerchunk-vs-NetCDF regression in data or metadata integrity.
- The observed discrepancies are concentrated in specific kerchunk JSON references or source-publication mismatches, rather than appearing as a systematic backend difference.
